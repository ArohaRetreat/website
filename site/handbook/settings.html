Settings reference

Find the settings for your account, this browser, or the retreat’s booking policy.

Guest settings

Account and visit history
Open the account menu from the website or booking page. Your verified sign-in email determines the bookings linked to your account.
Identity document
Use your booking account to upload or replace your document for front-desk verification. Only authorized staff can verify it.
Privacy preferences
Use the website’s privacy controls to manage optional monitoring and analytics consent. Browser preferences apply to that browser.

Host Studio settings

Sign-in accounts in Guests requires an active admin role with booking-manager access.

Appearance
Open Settings in the Studio menu. Light mode, Forest, Google Sans headings and Source Sans 3 body text are the defaults. Changes are saved for your Studio account in the current browser.
Booking rules
Open Cottages & Spaces and select Booking rules. Authorized staff can edit capacity, published rates and visit times. New requests use the updated rules and existing reservations keep their recorded terms.
Rooms and dates
Use Cottages, Facilities and Date blocks to manage what guests can request. Review availability before confirming a booking.

Integration access

Open Developer Connect and sign in to issue or revoke a token. Choose the environment, scopes and expiry based on that client’s work. Store the credential privately in the client’s configuration.
