Product handbook

Plan your visit, find your booking details or run the front desk in Host Studio.

Plan a visit ↗

For guests

  1. 1. Choose your visitExplore the Living Map, then check dates and rates for a stay or day visit.
  2. 2. Request a bookingSign in with Google, choose dates and guest numbers, review the quote and submit the request. The hosts confirm availability.
  3. 3. Open Your visitsCheck your booking status and available documents. Sign in with the same verified email the hosts used for a walk-in booking.
Living Map ↗ Guest settings

For hosts

Requests and arrivals
Start in the Studio dashboard. Review incoming requests, confirm or decline, then record arrivals and departures.
Walk-ins and guest history
Search for an existing guest or enter their own contact details. Review dates and the quote before confirming a manual booking.
Payments and documents
Record verified payments in the ledger. Open the booking or guest profile to find available documents.
Email follow-up
Check the provider status in Messages or the email history. A sent request, a confirmed booking and a delivered email each have their own status.
