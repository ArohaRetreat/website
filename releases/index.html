Production

Updates available on aroharetreat.com

  1. v0.33.1 10 November 2026

    Clearer feedback at the front desk

    Hosts see progress during booking actions and can keep reading the dashboard as it refreshes. Host Studio also supports early checkout.

    Booking API 0.18.0

    Publication record
  2. v0.32.3 10 November 2026

    Natura media moves to Firebase Storage

    The Natura design pages keep their original links while photos and other media load from Firebase Storage.

    Publication record
  3. v0.32.2 9 November 2026

    Aroha branding in Host Studio

    Host Studio shows the Aroha Retreat logo and keeps legal links visible at the bottom of the workspace.

    Publication record
  4. v0.32.1 9 November 2026

    A dashboard for the day’s work

    Studio opens on arrivals, departures and booking requests. Hosts can find guest profiles and review payments, invoices and sent emails. Guests can download issued invoices and receipts.

    Publication record
  5. v0.31.2 9 November 2026

    Saved IDs for future check-ins

    Guests can keep a newly saved ID photo for use on later visits.

    Publication record
  6. v0.31.1 9 November 2026

    Private identity review and guest numbers

    Guests can manage private ID photos, and booking managers can review them from active bookings. The release adds guest numbering and improves booking details on smaller screens.

    Publication record
  7. v0.30.3 8 November 2026

    A lighter website download

    Public retreat media moved to Firebase Storage, reducing the size of the files shipped with the website.

    Publication record
  8. v0.9.0 2 November 2026

    Explore the Living Map

    The Living Map introduced an illustrated farm route with eleven places to explore and a Reading View.
